KPAP Partner

We are proud to be one of only 66 local Tanzanian operators and one of just 168 operators worldwide recognized as an official KPAP (Kilimanjaro Porters Assistance Project) Partner, out of more than 2,100 operators on Kilimanjaro.

66 local operators | 168 worldwide | Out of 2,100+ total operators

This status means we commit to the highest standards for porter welfare: fair wages, proper equipment, weight limits, adequate food and shelter, and transparent tipping. These standards are independently monitored by KPAP on every climb.

For our clients, this translates directly into safer, better-organized, and higher-quality climbs. A well-treated, motivated mountain crew delivers a smoother, more reliable, and more enjoyable Kilimanjaro experience.

By climbing with us, you choose an ethical, responsible adventure that respects the people behind your success — without compromising on quality, safety, or professionalism.

Proud Member of TATO

We are a registered and active member of TATO (Tanzania Association of Tour Operators), the official umbrella organization representing professional tour operators in Tanzania.

TATO membership is not automatic. It confirms that we operate legally, transparently, and in compliance with national tourism regulations, including licensing, insurance requirements, safety standards, and ethical business practices.

Legal • Transparent • Compliant with national tourism regulations

For our guests, this means reliability, accountability, and professional service from the first inquiry to the end of your journey. For Tanzania, it means supporting responsible tourism that benefits local communities and protects natural heritage.

Choosing a TATO member means choosing a trusted, recognized, and locally rooted operator — not an unregulated middleman.
